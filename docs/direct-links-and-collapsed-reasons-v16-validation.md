## Collapsed exclusions

Confirmed failures appear before the whole-card disclosure with labels and comparison details. Semantically identical facts are deduplicated across quotations and units. A common failure across every offered supply is preferred as the whole-notice explanation; otherwise up to three failures appear with a remaining count. A verified competition closure has its own reason. Missing personal facts and unavailable source clauses never become exclusion reasons.

Default closed/gray cards retain title, address, official/detail links and now the actual exclusion reason. Possible, mixed and unresolved notices stay expanded. Expanded schedules, price data, detailed evidence and independent supply comparisons are preserved.
